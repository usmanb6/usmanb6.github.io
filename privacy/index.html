Privacy Policy

Fast Currency Converter · Last updated 27 October 2026

Fast Currency Converter (“the app”) is made by Usman Bhana (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: there is no account, I don't collect your name or email, and your settings stay on your phone. The free version shows ads from Google, which uses some data as described below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your chosen currencies, favourites, custom rates, settings (such as key sound and haptics) and the most recent exchange rates on your device, so it works offline. This never leaves your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Finding your local currency (IP address)

To show the currency of the country you're in, the app sends a request to country.is, which returns the country of your internet (IP) address. Your IP address is necessarily seen by that service to answer the request. The app uses only the country code it gets back and doesn't store or share your IP address. The app does not use GPS or ask for location permission.

Exchange rates

Rates are downloaded from Frankfurter, a free public rates service. The request contains no personal information, though, like any website, the service sees your IP address when the app connects.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and information about your device and how you interact with ads. If you're in the UK, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, you're asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

The one-time “Remove ads” purchase is handled by Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iPhone). I don't receive or store your payment details. The app only checks with the store whether you've bought it, so ads can be turned off and the purchase restored on a new phone.

What the app doesn't do

  • No account, sign-in or analytics of my own
  • No crash reporting
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location
  • I don't sell your personal information

Children

The app is a general-audience tool and isn't directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under UK or EU data protection law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its use. Because I don't hold personal data about you, most requests are best directed to Google for advertising data. You can still contact me with any question and I'll help where I can. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

US residents

Some US state privacy laws (for example in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and Utah) treat sharing data with ad partners for personalised ads as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. You can opt out at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices. Ads will still show, but they won't be personalised. You may also have the right to know what data is collected and to ask for it to be deleted; see “Your rights” above.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
