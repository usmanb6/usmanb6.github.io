Fast Currency Converter

How does the app choose my currencies?

The top currency is your home currency, from your phone's region setting. The bottom one is the currency of the country you're in now, worked out from your internet connection. If they're the same, it shows US dollars (or euros if your home currency is the dollar). Tap either currency to change it.

Changing currencies

Tap a currency to choose another. Search by name or code, for example “yen” or “JPY”. Use the swap button to switch them round.

Favourites

On the currency list, long-press a currency to add it to or remove it from Favourites. Favourites appear at the top of the list.

Calculator

The keypad does sums as you type: + − × ÷ and %. For example, type 45 × 3 to convert three £45 tickets. C clears; long-press delete to clear too.

Copy and paste

Long-press the converted amount to copy it. The menu also has Copy result and Paste amount.

Custom rates

Got a rate from your bank or exchange bureau? Open the menu → Custom rates and enter it. It's used for that pair of currencies (both ways) until you choose Reset to live rate. A “Custom rate” label shows while it's on.

How up to date are the rates?

Rates are reference rates updated once each working day, and the line under the converter shows when they were last updated. Tap the refresh button to check for new ones. Your bank or card provider will use its own rate and fees, so the amount you're charged may differ.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app keeps the last rates it downloaded. If it has never been online, it uses rates built into the app and shows “Offline rates”.

Removing ads

Go to Settings → Remove ads for a one-time purchase. Reinstalled or got a new phone? Open the same screen and tap Restore purchase, signed in to the same Google Play (or Apple) account.

Ad privacy choices

To change whether you see personalised ads, go to Settings → Privacy choices. See the Privacy Policy for details.
